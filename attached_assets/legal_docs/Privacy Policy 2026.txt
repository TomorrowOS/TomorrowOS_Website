1. About this Privacy Policy
TomorrowOS is an open-source foundation for building digital signage software.
This Privacy Policy explains how TomorrowOS Pty Ltd, trading as TomorrowOS, collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you:
visit the TomorrowOS website;
read or use our documentation;
contact us;
subscribe to updates;
participate in our community;
submit a project or contribution;
report a security issue;
interact with our GitHub organisation or other linked services; or
otherwise communicate with us.
In this policy, “TomorrowOS,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to:
TomorrowOS Pty Ltd ACN:TBD ABN: TBD Registered address: TBD Australia Email: hello@tomorrowos.org
This policy applies to information handled by TomorrowOS as an organisation. It does not govern information independently collected by GitHub, hosting providers, analytics providers, community platforms or other third-party services under their own privacy policies.
Australian organisations covered by the Privacy Act must maintain a clearly expressed and current privacy policy describing matters including the information collected, purposes, access and correction processes, complaints and likely overseas disclosures.
2. Our role
Depending on the context, TomorrowOS may act as:
a data controller or equivalent entity when we decide why and how information is handled through our website, mailing list, community or enquiries; or
a service provider or processor where we handle information strictly on behalf of another organisation under a separate written agreement.
The open-source TomorrowOS SDK and runtime ordinarily run within infrastructure selected and controlled by the developer or organisation using them. Information handled inside a third party’s own TomorrowOS-based product is generally controlled by that third party, not by TomorrowOS.

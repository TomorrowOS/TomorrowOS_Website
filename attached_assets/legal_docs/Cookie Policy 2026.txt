3. Cookie Policy
Effective date: 20/06/2026Last updated: 20/06/2026
1. About this Cookie Policy
This Cookie Policy explains how TomorrowOS uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and documentation.
“Cookies” includes, where relevant:
browser cookies;
local storage;
pixels;
tags;
SDK storage;
similar technologies that store or access information on a device.
2. What cookies are
Cookies are small data files placed on or read from your device when you use a website.
They can be used to:
operate a website;
remember choices;
protect services;
measure usage;
diagnose problems;
support embedded content;
provide advertising.
3. Categories we use
Strictly necessary
Required for the website to operate or to provide a feature you request.
Examples:
security;
load balancing;
consent choices;
form operation;
session continuity;
abuse prevention.
These cannot usually be disabled through our settings.
Preferences
Remember optional choices, such as:
theme;
language;
documentation preferences;
display settings.
These should remain disabled until consent where required.
Analytics
Help us understand:
which pages are used;
where visitors encounter errors;
how documentation performs;
broad traffic patterns.
Analytics should only activate after consent in jurisdictions requiring it.
Functional and embedded content
Enable optional third-party features, such as:
videos;
interactive examples;
GitHub widgets;
embedded community content.
These may allow the third party to collect information.
Marketing
Used for advertising, campaign measurement or cross-site tracking.
Recommended MVP position:
TomorrowOS does not currently use marketing or behavioural-advertising cookies.
Do not include a marketing category in the live banner unless marketing technology is actually installed.

5. Your choices
You can:
accept all optional technologies;
reject all non-essential technologies;
choose categories individually;
withdraw consent at any time.
Use the Cookie Settings link in the footer.
Withdrawing consent will not invalidate processing that occurred before withdrawal.
Some features may not work as expected if optional technologies are disabled.
6. Browser controls
You can also block or delete cookies using browser settings.
Blocking all cookies may affect essential website functionality.
7. Third-party technologies
Third-party services may set their own cookies when activated.
Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.
We will not activate non-essential third-party embeds before consent where consent is legally required.
8. Global Privacy Control
Recommended if analytics or advertising could qualify as “sharing”:
Where legally required and technically supported, we recognise Global Privacy Control or another valid browser-based opt-out signal.
If TomorrowOS uses no advertising and does not sell or share data, state:
TomorrowOS does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
9. Changes
We may update this Cookie Policy when technologies or providers change.
We may request consent again when changes materially affect previous choices.

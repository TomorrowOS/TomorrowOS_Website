# Design objective

The TomorrowOS Blog should feel like a serious engineering publication.

The visual direction should combine:

- an old-school technical journal;
- a carefully typeset Word document;
- a readable RFC;
- an academic paper;
- modern developer documentation;
- the existing TomorrowOS monochrome brand.

The experience must remain recognisably TomorrowOS.

It should not feel like:

- a generic SaaS blog;
- a colourful marketing landing page;
- a dashboard;
- a literal Microsoft Word clone;
- a magazine;
- a card-heavy article template.

The content, typography and evidence should lead the design.
